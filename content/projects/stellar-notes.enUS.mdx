Notes with structure: workspaces, hierarchy and a search that finds the note even when you cannot remember the exact word you used.

## Hybrid search

Semantic search alone fails on proper nouns and code; text search alone fails on synonyms. Both run together and the results are merged by rank, not by raw score — the scores live on different scales and adding them compares things that are not comparable.

## Document as a tree, not as HTML

The note is stored as a tree of blocks. Storing HTML saves work early and charges for it later, when it is time to diff, collaborate or export to another format.
